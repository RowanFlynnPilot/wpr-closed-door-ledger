The Closed Door Ledger

Every closed session that central Wisconsin governmental bodies noticed in their online agendas, the exemption each notice cites under Wis. Stat. § 19.85(1), and the notice's exact wording.

What the counts mean

Every count is of closed sessions noticed in the body's online agenda. A posted agenda isn't necessarily the legal notice: s. 19.84(1) is also met by physical posting and by telling the news media. And a session that was noticed may never have been held. Coverage: the bodies listed in bodies.yaml.

Bodies

Each committee, commission and board is its own governmental body under s. 19.82(1), so each has its own row. Grouped by jurisdiction and listed alphabetically; nothing is ranked.

Exemptions cited

Every notice

One row per closed-session item, newest first, in the notice's own words. Each links to the agenda on the body's site and to the ledger's archived copy, which is kept even if the body later changes or removes its agenda.
